About Me

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Technology at Nankai University, advised by Prof. Qibin Hou. I received my M.S. degree from the MAC Lab, Institute of Artificial Intelligence, Xiamen University, advised by Prof. Xiaoshuai Sun. Prior to that, I obtained my B.S. degree in Statistics from Wendian College, Anhui University. My research interests include multimodal learning, 3D perception, and multimodal large language models.
