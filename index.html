About Me

I am currently an Algorithm Engineer in the AI Search & Multimodal Understanding Group at Kuaishou Technology, working on multimodal search and retrieval systems.

I received my M.S. degree from the Institute of Artificial Intelligence, Xiamen University (2022–2025), advised by Prof. Xiaoshuai Sun. Prior to that, I obtained my B.S. degree in Statistics from Anhui University (2018–2022), ranking 3rd out of 24 with a GPA of 3.95.

My research interests lie in multimodal learning, 3D perception, and multimodal large language models. I have published papers at top venues including AAAI, ECCV, EMNLP, and ACL.

CSMCIR: CoT-Enhanced Symmetric Alignment with Memory Bank for Composed Image Retrieval
Zhipeng Qian, Xiaoshuai Sun, et al.
ACL 2026 Findings
A unified representation framework that addresses fragmented representation spaces in composed image retrieval via multi-level CoT prompting, symmetric dual-tower architecture (shared Q-Former), and entropy-based dynamic memory bank.

UniECS: Unified Multimodal E-Commerce Search Framework with Gated Cross-modal Fusion
Zhipeng Qian, et al.
CIKM 2025
A flexible unified architecture for diverse e-commerce retrieval scenarios (image, text, and composed modalities) via a single gated cross-modal fusion pipeline, eliminating the need for separate task-specific models.
Paper Code
[3]
AnyTrans: Translate AnyText in the Image with Large Scale Models
Zhipeng Qian, Xiaoshuai Sun, et al.
EMNLP 2024 Findings
A fully training-free image translation pipeline leveraging open-source models. The first work to introduce VLLM and diffusion models into image translation, outperforming Apple iOS and Microsoft Translator on human and GPT-4o evaluations.
Paper Code
[2]
Multi-branch Collaborative Learning Network for 3D Visual Grounding
Zhipeng Qian, Xiaoshuai Sun, et al.
ECCV 2024
Dual-branch collaborative network (MCLN) with RSA and ASA modules for joint 3D Referring Expression Comprehension (3DREC) and Segmentation (3DRES). Achieves +2.05% Acc@0.5 on 3DREC and +3.96% mIoU on 3DRES.
Paper Code
[1]
X-RefSeg3D: Enhancing Referring 3D Instance Segmentation via Structured Cross-Modal Graph Neural Networks
Zhipeng Qian, Xiaoshuai Sun, et al.
AAAI 2024
Addresses the 3D Referring Expression Segmentation task via POS-based word classification, chain reasoning for feature weighting, and staged graph convolution for relative spatial relationships. Improves mIoU by 3.67% while halving memory usage.
