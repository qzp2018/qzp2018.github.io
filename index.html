About Me

I am currently an Algorithm Engineer in the AI Search & Multimodal Understanding Group at Kuaishou Technology, working on multimodal search and retrieval systems.

I received my M.S. degree from the MAC Lab, Institute of Artificial Intelligence, Xiamen University (2022–2025), advised by Prof. Xiaoshuai Sun. Prior to that, I obtained my B.S. degree in Statistics from Anhui University (2018–2022), ranking 3rd out of 24 with a GPA of 3.95.

My research interests include multimodal learning, 3D perception, and multimodal large language models. I have published papers at AAAI, ECCV, EMNLP, CIKM, and ACL.
